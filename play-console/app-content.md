# Google Play app content

Use this file when completing Play Console. Recheck each answer against the
release bundle before submission.

## Data safety

1. Does the app collect or share required user data types? No.
2. Is user data encrypted in transit? Not applicable; the app sends no data.
3. Can users request data deletion? No account or remote data exists. Clearing
   app data or uninstalling removes local settings.
4. Does the app use third-party SDKs that collect data? No.
5. Privacy policy: publish `PRIVACY.md` at a public HTTPS URL and enter that
   URL in Play Console.

## Other declarations

1. Ads: No.
2. App access: All features are available without an account or login.
3. Target audience: Select ages 13 and older unless the app is changed for
   children.
4. News app: No.
5. Government app: No.
6. Financial features: None.
7. Health features: None.
8. Content rating: Utility app; answer no to violence, sexual content,
   language, controlled substances, gambling, and user interaction.

## Permissions for reviewers

1. Display over other apps is required for the core dimming filter.
2. Notifications are required for the ongoing Stop control.
3. The light sensor is optional and is used only on the device for bright-light
   auto-stop.
