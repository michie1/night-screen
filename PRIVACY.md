# Night Screen privacy policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Night Screen does not collect, send, sell, or share personal data.

## Data kept on the device

Night Screen stores these settings on the device:

1. Chosen brightness.
2. Whether the blue-light filter is enabled.
3. The chosen blue-light filter strength.
4. Whether bright-light auto-stop is enabled.
5. The chosen bright-light level.

The optional light sensor reading is used on the device. It is not saved or
sent anywhere. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes its saved
settings.

## Permissions

Night Screen uses:

1. Display over other apps, to place the dimming filter on the screen.
2. Notifications, to show the ongoing dimming control and Stop action.
3. A foreground service, to keep user-started dimming active.

Night Screen has no internet permission, analytics, advertising, accounts, or
third-party data collection SDKs.

## Changes

If this policy changes, its effective date and text will be updated before the
new app version is released.
