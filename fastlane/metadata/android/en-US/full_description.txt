Night Screen adds a dark filter that can dim your display below Android's
normal minimum brightness.

Use the live slider to find a comfortable level from 0.1% to 100%. Dimming
continues when you leave the app, with a clear Stop action in the ongoing
notification.

Features:

• Live brightness control
• Compact floating slider while dimming
• Blue-light filter with adjustable strength
• Optional bright-light auto-stop
• No ads, accounts, analytics, or internet access
• Settings stay on your device

Night Screen needs permission to display over other apps so its filter can
cover the screen. It also needs notifications so the active dimming service
always has a visible Stop control.

Very dark filters can cause Android to block taps in other apps. The ongoing
notification provides a backup Stop action.
